Training principles

Resistance training can support strength, muscle, functional capacity, and healthy aging. Progress gradually, train major movement patterns, and choose sustainable workloads.

Strength

Heavier, controlled sets with appropriate rest can develop force production.

General capacity

Moderate loads and varied rep ranges support broad training goals.

Muscular endurance

Repeated contractions with manageable resistance build task-specific endurance.

Movement quality

Use a comfortable range of motion and prioritize technique and control.

These categories overlap. A repetition range alone does not determine an exercise's effect. Seek qualified guidance for injuries or medical restrictions.

Movement patterns

Squat · Hinge · Push · Pull · Carry/core. Select exercises that fit your equipment and ability. These patterns are a coverage framework, not a rigid quota.
